Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

ARIGroup ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ARIGroup mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use ARIGroup:

  • Camera access: The App uses your device camera to scan QR codes on restaurant menus. Camera data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Account information: If you create an account or post on the marketplace, we collect your username and content you choose to share.
  • Usage data: We may collect information on how the App is accessed and used, including device type, operating system, and app interactions.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App
  • To allow QR code scanning to display augmented reality menu content
  • To enable marketplace functionality for restaurant suppliers
  • To improve the App and user experience

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. AR content and menu data are retrieved from our servers to display restaurant products. We use standard cloud hosting services to operate the App.

Camera Permission

ARIGroup requests camera access solely to scan QR codes embedded in restaurant menus. No images or video are recorded or transmitted. Camera data is used only to identify the QR code and load the corresponding AR content.

Data Retention

We retain marketplace posts and account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

ARIGroup is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.
