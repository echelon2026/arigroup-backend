Delete Your ARIGroup Account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

You may request deletion of your ARIGroup account and all associated data at any time. Please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to hello@arigroup.space with the subject line "Delete Account Request".
  2. Include the username or email address associated with your account so we can identify it.
  3. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by email.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon deletion of your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your account profile (username, email address)
  • All marketplace posts and listings you have created
  • All replies and messages associated with your account

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for a limited period to comply with legal obligations or to prevent fraud. Retained data will be securely deleted once the retention period expires.

Contact Us

If you have questions about account deletion, contact us at: hello@arigroup.space
